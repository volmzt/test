1. General information

YANCHYNNYA MUSIC is an independent music project founded on 15 November 2024 by two authors who perform under the public pseudonyms volmzt and OSRSS. The project arose from their first experiments in creating and processing music and gradually grew into an independent creative initiative with its own name, a catalogue of compositions, an official website and a presence on several digital platforms.

Since its foundation, YANCHYNNYA MUSIC has developed as an independent project in which making music became a way of experimenting with sound, processing, atmosphere and various ideas. At first, music production was not tied to commercial or professional goals. The main line of activity was the independent creation of musical material and the search for approaches that would turn individual experiments into finished compositions.

As the project developed, the participants began to pay more attention to the structure of the works, sound processing, the presentation of musical releases and the organisation of the overall catalogue. Gradually, YANCHYNNYA MUSIC acquired a history of its own, including early experiments, published compositions, remixes, demo versions and alternative versions.

The project is not limited to one strictly defined musical genre. Its catalogue brings together various works created at different stages of its development and reflects the experimental nature of its music production. It contains original compositions, remixes, demo versions, slowed-down versions (Slowed), sped-up versions (SpeedUp) and other variants of processing the musical material.

An important part of the project is the preservation of its musical history. Early works remain part of the overall catalogue alongside later compositions, so the development of YANCHYNNYA MUSIC can be seen as a sequence of experiments, changes in production and the gradual formation of its own creative identity.

As of October 2026, the project’s catalogue comprises 25 musical positions, counting the main compositions, remixes, demo versions and alternative versions. The catalogue continues to develop as new works appear.

2. History of the project

2.1. Foundation and first experiments

YANCHYNNYA MUSIC was founded on 15 November 2024 by two participants, volmzt and OSRSS. This date is regarded as the starting point of the project’s history and marks the beginning of a joint creative initiative that later acquired its own name, a musical catalogue and a digital presence.

At the initial stage, making music was above all an experimental process. The participants tried various ways of working with sound, explored the capabilities of the software available to them and gradually gained experience of independent music production. During that period, the main attention was given to the process of creating compositions itself, rather than to building a full-fledged musical brand.

One of the main tools of the early period was CapCut. The programme was used to create and process the first musical works. The available tools made it possible to experiment with audio material, change its sound and create compositions without the need for complex professional production tools.

The early works were distinguished by comparatively simple methods of processing and a less developed sound. However, they were important for the formation of the project: it was in the course of these experiments that the participants’ initial ideas about making music, processing sound and the possibilities of further development took shape.

The composition ZOMBIE belongs to the early period and is considered the first work created within YANCHYNNYA MUSIC. However, ZOMBIE was not the project’s first full official release. This distinction is important for understanding the chronology: the first composition created and the first full work published do not necessarily coincide.

Despite the technical limitations and the experimental nature of early production, the initial stage laid the foundation for the further development of YANCHYNNYA MUSIC. It became the starting point for the appearance of subsequent compositions and for the gradual transition from individual musical experiments to a more organised project.

2.2. The transition from experiments to systematic music-making

As experience accumulated, making music became more systematic. The participants began to regard compositions not only as independent experiments but also as parts of a single catalogue united under the common name YANCHYNNYA MUSIC.

Attention to sound processing, the structure of the works and the overall perception of the finished compositions gradually increased. The approach to musical material also changed: individual works were kept as part of the project’s history, while some compositions received additional versions and new variants of processing.

In the early period, a significant part of the musical material was created by OSRSS. Later, volmzt became the main author of many new compositions. Thus the distribution of creative tasks changed as the project developed, and the participants’ contributions differed depending on the period and the particular work.

The compositions associated with volmzt include SAD, YANCHALKA IN THE AREA, B.O.M.J and VALID0L. The works associated with OSRSS include SKYFALL, RONALDO and KLITCHATKA. These works form part of the catalogue through which the authors’ participation in the development of the project’s musical direction can be traced.

The development of YANCHYNNYA MUSIC was not simply a matter of making the sound of each new composition ever more complex. Different works could differ in their approach to creation, in the nature of their processing and in their degree of experimentation. An important feature remained the possibility of trying different solutions and keeping the results in the common catalogue.

Over time, the project came to be perceived as an independent musical initiative with its own history, its own name and a set of works. This created the basis for the subsequent development of the official website and of the presence of YANCHYNNYA MUSIC on digital platforms.

2.3. The origin of the name YANCHYNNYA

The name YANCHYNNYA has an everyday origin and is connected with the Ukrainian colloquial expression “шо ты янчиш” (“sho ty yanchysh”).

According to the recollections of one of the founders, this expression was used in his family, including by his grandmother, in situations when someone was shouting loudly or making unusual sounds. The word “янчиш” (“yanchysh”) subsequently became the basis of the name YANCHYNNYA.

Thus the name of the project came about naturally and was not the result of developing a professional musical brand or a prepared marketing concept. Its origin lies in an everyday expression that, over time, acquired a new meaning in the context of musical activity.

The unusual spelling of the name sets YANCHYNNYA MUSIC apart from more traditional names of music projects. It is used in the name of the project, on the official website and in its digital presence, uniting the published compositions and materials under a single identity.

The name YANCHYNNYA MUSIC should be regarded as the proper name of an independent music project. It denotes the project as a whole, rather than an individual composition or a specific musical genre.

4. Musical style and creative approach

YANCHYNNYA MUSIC does not adhere to a strictly defined musical genre. Experimentation is one of the project’s main characteristics and is reflected in the variety of compositions, the methods of processing and the existence of alternative versions.

The musical catalogue took shape gradually, so works from different periods may differ noticeably from one another. The early compositions reflect the first attempts at making music, whereas the later works appeared after a certain amount of experience had been accumulated and approaches to production had changed.

The YANCHYNNYA MUSIC catalogue contains the following categories:

Original compositions — independent musical works that form the basis of the catalogue.

Remixes — alternative versions of existing musical material in which the original work receives a different variant of processing.

Demo versions — experimental or preliminary variants of musical works.

Slowed — versions in which playback is slowed down to change the tempo and the overall perception of the composition.

SpeedUp — sped-up versions that change the tempo and character of the sound of the original material.

Other alternative versions — additional variants of works that differ in processing or in the manner of presentation.

The existence of several versions of one composition is part of the catalogue structure of the project. Such versions do not necessarily constitute separate original works: they may relate to a single original composition and show different ways of processing it.

Within YANCHYNNYA MUSIC, the approach in which individual works receive several variants is particularly noticeable. This makes it possible to keep different versions of the musical material in the catalogue and to regard them as parts of the history of a specific composition.

At the same time, the presence of Slowed, SpeedUp, remixes and demo versions does not mean that the whole project is limited to processing existing music. The catalogue also includes independent compositions created at various periods in the existence of YANCHYNNYA MUSIC.

4. Музыкальный стиль и творческий подход

YANCHYNNYA MUSIC не придерживается строго определённого музыкального жанра. Экспериментальность является одной из основных характеристик проекта и проявляется в разнообразии композиций, методах обработки и наличии альтернативных версий.

Оригинальные композиции — самостоятельные музыкальные произведения, составляющие основу каталога.

Ремиксы — альтернативные версии существующего музыкального материала, в которых исходное произведение получает другой вариант обработки.

Демоверсии — экспериментальные или предварительные варианты музыкальных работ.

Slowed — версии, в которых воспроизведение замедлено для изменения темпа и общего восприятия композиции.

SpeedUp — ускоренные версии, изменяющие темп и характер звучания исходного материала.

Другие альтернативные версии — дополнительные варианты произведений, отличающиеся обработкой или способом представления.

Наличие нескольких версий одной композиции является частью каталожной структуры проекта. Такие версии не обязательно представляют собой отдельные оригинальные произведения: они могут относиться к одной исходной композиции и показывать различные способы её обработки.

В рамках YANCHYNNYA MUSIC особенно заметен подход, при котором отдельные произведения получают несколько вариантов. Это позволяет сохранять разные версии музыкального материала в каталоге и рассматривать их как части истории конкретной композиции.

При этом наличие Slowed, SpeedUp, ремиксов и демоверсий не означает, что весь проект ограничивается обработкой уже существующей музыки. Каталог также включает самостоятельные композиции, созданные в разные периоды существования YANCHYNNYA MUSIC.
